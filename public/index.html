The order

The book

Where does this money actually live? Show me▾

This whole book lives on Ethereum. You never installed a wallet, never bought a coin, never paid a fee, never saw a secret phrase. It still happened on a public chain that nobody — not us — can quietly edit.

Your phone holds a key it made when you signed in. That key can't pay fees, so it just signs a note saying what you authorised. Our relayer carries the note to the chain and pays the fee. You approved it. Somebody else paid the postage.

The order is enforced by the contract, not by us. There is no function anywhere that lets anybody choose who gets paid. The contract works it out from the order locked in when the circle started.

Test money on Ethereum's Sepolia test network. Not audited. The key sits in this browser rather than being split across servers, which a real deployment would do. Balances are issued by us — there is no mobile money connection yet.
