This whole book lives on Ethereum. You never installed a wallet, never bought a coin, never paid a fee, never saw a secret phrase. It still happened on a public chain that nobody — not us — can quietly edit.

Your phone holds a key it made when you signed in. That key can't pay fees, so it just signs a note saying what you authorised. Our relayer carries the note to the chain and pays the fee. You approved it. Somebody else paid the postage.
